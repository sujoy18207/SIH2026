# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Government officers monitoring the MPLADS scheme: MoSPI national officers, State Nodal Authorities, District Collectors, and MPs' offices. They open the dashboard at work, triage risk alerts over 128,670 real eSAKSHI works, drill into investigation dossiers, and record officer reviews. Secondary audience: SIH 2026 judges evaluating the platform live on a projector, typically seeing the dashboard for the first time with no hand-holding.

## Product Purpose

An AI-powered anomaly, fraud, and inefficiency detection platform for MPLADS. It never accuses; every finding is a scored "Potential Anomaly / Verification Required" with multi-signal evidence, guiding officers on where to direct field verification. Success = an officer can scan the national picture in seconds, drill into any flagged work, and act on it — and a judge can follow the same path unassisted.

## Brand Personality

Official, precise, trustworthy. Feels like a genuine Government of India digital service (eSAKSHI-lineage branding: navy, teal, tricolor accents) with modern data-intelligence craft. Three words: authoritative, calm, legible.

## Anti-references

- Startup-y SaaS dashboards (glassmorphism, gradient text, purple-tinted dark modes, bouncy motion) — would read as unserious to ministry judges.
- Cluttered legacy government portals (misaligned tables, 1990s link-blue, walls of raw data) — the platform sells signal, not noise.
- The current state's seam: two stitched themes with conflicting risk-color tokens and mismatched components.

## Design Principles

1. **Evidence over decoration** — every pixel that isn't data or affordance earns its place; the dataset (128K works, ₹3,969 Cr) is the hero, not the chrome.
2. **One portal, one system** — a single token set and component language across all tabs; no theme seams.
3. **Scannable in seconds** — an officer (or judge) should grasp the national risk picture from the doorway of the room: hierarchy, color coding, and numbers lead.
4. **Verification-first framing** — colors and copy reinforce "potential anomaly / verification required," never accusation.
5. **Government-native but not dated** — respect eSAKSHI portal conventions (light content, official navy/teal, tricolor accents) while executing at modern product craft.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1, full keyboard navigation for tables/modals/drawer, visible focus states, prefers-reduced-motion respected for the map pulses and reveals. Color-coded risk levels always paired with text labels (never color alone).
